Support

Answers to common questions. For anything else, email support@vaulora.xyz. Never include your PIN, password or recovery key in an email.

Unlocking

I forgot my PIN or password

On the lock screen, tap Forgot and enter your recovery key, the 25-character key shown when you created the vault. You'll then choose a new PIN or password. Without the recovery key there's no way back in: your vault is encrypted and nobody, including the developer, holds a copy of the key.

Why do I have to wait after wrong attempts?

After five wrong attempts in a row, Vaulora makes you wait before trying again, like the iPhone passcode: 1 minute, then 5, then 15, then an hour. Changing the date doesn't shorten the wait.

Face ID stopped working

If you add or remove a face or fingerprint in the Settings app, Vaulora turns biometric unlock off for safety. Unlock with your PIN, then turn it back on in Vaulora's settings.

Backups and moving to a new device

How do I move my vault to a new iPhone?

Three ways: turn on iCloud Sync and choose Restore from iCloud when setting up Vaulora on the new device; restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one; or export a backup (Settings → Backup) and restore it on the new device. In every case you unlock with the same PIN, password or recovery key.

Can I open a backup without Vaulora?

Yes. Backups are ordinary encrypted .7z or .zip archives. They open with 7-Zip, Keka, WinRAR, ZArchiver and similar apps, using the password you set when exporting.

Features

What is a decoy vault?

A second, separate vault with its own PIN. If you're pressured to open Vaulora, enter the decoy PIN: it unlocks normally and shows only what you put in it. Set it up in Settings → Decoy Vault. Nothing on the device shows that a decoy exists.

How do break-in photos work?

If you turn them on (Settings → Break-in Photos), Vaulora takes a photo with the front camera after the number of wrong attempts you choose, and shows it next time you unlock, with the time. The photo is stored encrypted in your vault.

Can I change Vaulora's icon or name?

Settings → App Icon offers a few alternative icons. iOS doesn't let apps change the name shown under the icon. On iOS 18 or later you can hide Vaulora: touch and hold its icon and choose Require Face ID → Hide and Require Face ID.

Are Live Photos kept?

Yes. Live Photos stay live when you import them, move them in and out of Vaulora, archive them or back them up, and their dates and locations are kept.
