Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

In short: Vaulora doesn't collect any data. The developer of Vaulora can't see what you store in it, who you are or how you use the app. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

Photos, videos, files, notes, folder and file names, settings and break-in photos are encrypted on your device with AES-256 before they are saved. The key is protected by your PIN or password (with Argon2id), by your recovery key, and, if you turn it on, by Face ID or Touch ID through the device's Keychain. Nobody can open your vault without one of these, including the developer.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Vaulora stores a copy of your vault in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. The copy is exactly as encrypted as it is on your device, so it can only be opened with your PIN, password or recovery key. The developer has no access to your iCloud data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn sync off and delete the iCloud copy in Vaulora's settings.

Backups and archives you create

When you export a backup or share files, Vaulora creates the file you asked for and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you. Backups and archives are protected by the password you choose.

Permissions

  • Photos: to import photos and videos you choose, delete the originals if you ask it to, and save items back to your library. Vaulora doesn't browse or upload your library.
  • Camera and microphone: to take photos and videos directly into the vault, and, if you turn on break-in photos, to take a photo with the front camera after wrong PIN entries. Those photos are stored encrypted in your vault.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to unlock the vault. Biometric data is handled by the operating system and never reaches Vaulora.

Things Vaulora doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect analytics, crash reports, device identifiers or usage data.
  • It doesn't include advertising or tracking code, or third-party code that collects data.
  • It doesn't make network connections other than Apple's iCloud (only if you turn on sync).

Children

Vaulora collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
